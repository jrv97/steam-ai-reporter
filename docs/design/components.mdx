# AI Reporter for Steam UI components (v1)

Component specs for everything the extension and the userscript inject into store pages or own outright. This folder mirrors the published design system (https://claude.ai/artifact/3xsXmaA5vjofhEWkL8nYsQ, private to the owner's account): change one, then sync the other. The UI step of `docs/plans/steam-ai-reporter-v1.md` (step 10) builds from this file, and the i18n step (step 4) authors `src/locales/en/messages.json` from the message catalog at the end.

**Files**

- `docs/design/tokens.css`: every `--sar-*` custom property. The only source of colors, sizes and fonts.
- `docs/design/reference.css`: reference rules for each component below, written against the tokens only. The UI step can lift them into `src/core/ui/styles.css` as they are.
- `docs/design/previews/`: one page per component, the same previews the design system shows, rendering the reference rules on sampled store grounds, synthetic cover art and long Spanish copy.
- `docs/design/specimen.html`: every preview on one page. Open it from disk to review a change.
- `assets/icons/`: the extension icon (section 14).
- `docs/design/decisions.mdx`: why each choice was made, and what is still open.

AI Reporter for Steam labels games on the Steam store whose developers filled in Steam's AI content disclosure, and quotes what they wrote. It ships as a browser extension and a userscript. Its UI lives in two places: small pieces injected into store pages (badges, a banner, a list filter) and its own settings pages (options page, toolbar popup, userscript dialog). This system covers both.

Every class, attribute and custom property starts with `sar-`.

## Principles

1. **Label the disclosure, never judge it.** A disclosure can cover one marketing image or live generated gameplay, and some notes say AI was barely used. Attribute every fact to the developer ("discloses", "named by the developer", "in the developer's own words"). No red, no warning triangles.
2. **Nothing for absence.** "Not disclosed" and "unknown" render nothing: no neutral badge, no check mark. Only `data-sar-state` on the host records them.
3. **Quote verbatim.** The developer's notes are data: set with `textContent` and `white-space: pre-line`, never translated, summarized or highlighted.
4. **Feel native.** Look and behave as close to the store's own UI as possible: its sizes, type, neutrals, surfaces and interaction patterns, so injected UI has no seam. Model each store-page component on its closest store element (the banner on the notice bubbles, the full badge on the tag chips, the compact badge on the corner flags, the summary row on the summary box's own rows). Only the violet hue marks a component as ours, so nobody mistakes it for a store feature.
5. **Never block.** No modals over store pages, no overlays over content, no focus stealing, no layout shift after a component's first paint.
6. **Copy lives in catalogs.** Components hold message keys, never text. Leave room for translations about 40% longer than English.
7. **Cheap at scale.** Express state with `data-sar-*` attributes and CSS, never per-node inline styles. A setting change is one attribute write on the root element.

## Voice and copy

- Say "discloses" or "disclosed". Never "uses AI", "AI-free" or "no AI", in any locale; the copy test fails the build on them.
- Plain, short, sentence case: "AI use disclosed", "Stop marking this game", "Show full disclosure".
- Name the developer as the source: "The developer discloses AI use", "In the developer's own words:", "AI service named by the developer:".
- "Stop marking this game", never "Ignore": the store has its own Ignore button just above.
- Settings speak about games, not switches: the list filter reads "Show", "Dim", "Hide".
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no em dashes.
- The full catalog, with translator notes, is in the Messages section.

## Color

One dark theme. Store pages are dark, and the settings pages open from them.

- **Neutrals are sampled.** `sar-color-bg-page`, `sar-color-bg-page-deep` and `sar-color-bg-row` are the store's own grounds: our components sit on them and never paint them. `sar-color-bg-panel`, `sar-color-bg-raised` and `sar-color-bg-sunken` are the settings pages' surfaces.
- **Text:** on the banner wash, everything is `sar-banner-fg`, the store's own bubble text color. Elsewhere, `sar-color-text-strong` for headings, `sar-color-text` for body copy, `sar-color-text-muted` for meta lines, `sar-color-text-subtle` for help and dates. Muted and subtle never go on the banner wash, and subtle never on the store's row hover.
- **Violet is ours.** Every other hue already means something on the store: blue is links, tags and the wishlist; green is buy, discount and owned; tan is the store's own third-party notices; red is live. Violet carries no traffic-light verdict, which matters because the badge must not judge.
- **The accent scale:** `sar-accent-100` for compact badge text, `sar-accent-200` for full badge text, `sar-accent-300` for links and the focus ring, `sar-accent-400` for non-text brand fills and edges (and the full badge tint at 20%), `sar-accent-800` and `sar-accent-600` at 80% for the banner wash, `sar-accent-600` and `sar-accent-700` for solid fills with white text, `sar-accent-900` for the compact badge's dark surface.
- **Error** is text, always with words, never a red fill: `sar-color-error` on extension pages, `sar-banner-error` inside the banner.
- Never set a store hue (blue, green, tan, red) to a new meaning.

Every text pair meets WCAG 2.2 AA on the grounds its token's note names; see the Contrast section.

## Type

- **Store pages:** the `page` family, which names the store's own "Motiva Sans" first. It is referenced by name only, never bundled; outside the store it falls back to Noto Sans or Arial.
- **The summary box:** the `summary` family (Arial), like the store's own summary rows and tag chips there.
- **Extension pages:** the `ext` family, a system UI stack. The store font is not available there.
- Sizes follow the store's 11, 12, 13, 15, 18 and 24px steps, plus 14/21 for quoted notes, the size the store sets its own disclosure section in. The banner is set light (300), like the store's own notices, and its notes italic, like the store's disclosure section. Use the named styles: `sar-text-badge`, `sar-text-compact`, `sar-text-compact-lg`, `sar-text-banner-title`, `sar-text-banner-meta`, `sar-text-banner-quote`, `sar-text-banner-button`, `sar-text-banner-note` on store pages; `sar-text-options-title`, `sar-text-card-title`, `sar-text-body`, `sar-text-help` on extension pages.
- Never shrink type to fit a translation; truncate a badge or let the block wrap.

## Space, radius, layering

- Spacing is the store's React scale: `sar-space-0` to `sar-space-6` (2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32px). Badges keep their own exact paddings (`sar-badge-pad-inline`, `sar-compact-pad-inline`).
- Radii are small or none, like the store's: none on the banner and the compact badge (like the notice bubbles and corner flags), `sar-radius-xs` (1px) on the allow-listed note, `sar-radius-sm` (2px) for the full badge, buttons and segments, `sar-radius-md` (4px) for cards on extension pages, `sar-radius-pill` only for the switch track.
- No shadows on store pages except `sar-compact-shadow`, which keeps a compact badge's edge over mid-tone art, and the hover card's, which copies the store's own popup shadow. The banner is flat, like the store's notice bubbles. Cards on extension pages are flat.
- `sar-z-badge` (4) sits over capsule art, the store's corner flags and its hover microtrailer video (z-index 3), under its hover popups.
- `sar-z-hovercard` (2100) sits over the store's game hover popup (1526) and its React capsule hover layer (2000), for our hover card (section 15).

## Motion and focus

- Animate only color and opacity, with `sar-duration-fast` (120ms) or `sar-duration-base` (180ms) and `sar-ease`, and never on many elements at once. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` sets both durations to 0ms.
- The banner's collapse does not animate: a jump is calmer than a fake slide.
- Focus is a solid `sar-focus-width` (2px) ring in `sar-focus-color` at `sar-focus-offset` (2px), on `:focus-visible` only. It holds 3.69:1 or more on every ground, the banner wash included. Badges are not focusable; they ride inside the store's own link, whose focus opens the hover card (section 15).
- Forced colors: badges switch to `Canvas` and `CanvasText` with a visible border; the banner, whose wash disappears, gets a `CanvasText` edge.

## Longer translations and direction

- Badges stay on one line and truncate past their cap; the full label stays in the DOM text screen readers read, and the hover card shows it in full.
- Everything else wraps. Nothing that holds copy gets a fixed height; controls with copy have a minimum block size.
- Use logical properties (`padding-inline`, `inset-inline-end`, `border-inline-start`, `text-align: start`). The one physical value is the switch thumb's `translateX`. Right-to-left is out of scope for v1, but nothing here assumes left or right.
- No text in icons or images.

## Iconography

- **The mark** is a label tag pointing toward inline-end with a four-point spark on its visual center: "a label about AI", not "an AI tool". One flat `sar-accent-400` fill with a white spark; no outline, no gradient, no letters. See the Icon asset group for files and sizes.
- **No icons in components.** Badges, the summary row and the banner carry no icon, like the store's own tags, corner flags and notice bubbles. The violet hue and the wording mark them as ours. The spark appears only in the extension icon.
- No other icon set. Platform icons and flags around our badges are the store's.
- No Valve logo, wordmark, icon, image or font file appears anywhere.

## What renders for each state

| Verdict | Full badge | Compact badge | Summary row | Banner | List filter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Disclosed | yes | yes | yes | full banner | dim or hide |
| Disclosed, allow-listed | nothing | nothing | nothing | one-line note | nothing |
| Not disclosed | nothing | nothing | nothing | nothing | nothing |
| Unknown | nothing | nothing | nothing | nothing | nothing |

Settings gate each column: `badges` off removes both badges and the summary row, `banner` off removes the banner and its note, `listFilter: "off"` leaves lists alone. The allow-list wins over every setting.

## Shared rules

**DOM ownership and state**

- Every injected element carries a class starting with `sar-`; every attribute we set on store nodes starts with `data-sar-`. The scanner skips both.
- Hosts get `data-sar-appid` and `data-sar-state` (`disclosed`, `not-disclosed`, `unknown`, `allowed`), plus `data-sar-hideable="false"` where the placement says hiding would break the list. These are written once, when the scanner marks the host.
- Settings live as attributes on `document.documentElement`: `data-sar-filter` (`off`, `dim`, `hide`) and `data-sar-badges` (`on`, `off`). The list filter and the live badge switch are pure CSS on top of them (sections 1, 2, 3 and 5).
- Never move, wrap or restyle store nodes. The store-node changes besides the attributes above are `data-sar-anchor` on an image container that computes to `position: static`, which a CSS rule turns into `position: relative` (section 2), and a transient `aria-describedby` on a focused store link while the hover card is open (section 15).
- DOM reads (image sizes, computed position) are batched before writes in one `requestAnimationFrame` per scan.
- Listeners are delegated: one per root, never one per node. The summary row's jump to the banner is one such listener (section 3); the hover card's are on the document (section 15).

**Style isolation**

- Light-DOM components (both badges, the summary row and the hover card) reset with `all: unset` on themselves and their descendants, then set every property they need. Store CSS styles bare `span`, `a`, `h2` and `button` in places, and `all` leaves custom properties alone, so tokens still reach them.
- The banner renders into a shadow root on a host `div.sar-banner-host`. Page CSS cannot reach it, and its CSS cannot leak. Tokens inherit into it from `:root`, and the page's "Motiva Sans" works inside it because `@font-face` is document-wide. Attach the stylesheet with `adoptedStyleSheets`; if the store's CSP or a script manager blocks that, fall back to light DOM plus the badge reset.
- The userscript settings dialog uses the same shadow-root approach.

**i18n**

- Every visible string, `title`, `aria-label` and live-region message comes from `t(key, params)`. CSS carries no copy (no `content:` strings), and `options.html` carries `data-i18n` keys only.
- Data is never translated and never goes through the catalog: the developer's notes, the named AI service, game names and app IDs.
- Dates use `Intl.DateTimeFormat(locale, { dateStyle: "medium" })`. Plurals use `_one` and `_other` keys chosen with `Intl.PluralRules`. Language names in the language picker use `Intl.DisplayNames`, each in its own language, not catalog entries.
- Uppercase is CSS (`text-transform`), never catalog text, so it follows the page's `lang`.
- Sizing for longer translations is checked in the previews with Spanish copy and with labels up to three times the English length.

## 1. Full badge

The text badge that labels a disclosed game on rows with room for words: search results and wishlist rows.

Preview: `previews/Badge.html`.

### When to use

- Only for a host whose `data-sar-state` is `disclosed`. Not disclosed, unknown and allow-listed hosts get nothing: no neutral badge, no check mark.
- One full badge per host. A host with a full badge gets no compact badge.

### What the consumer provides

```html
<span class="sar-badge">
  <span class="sar-badge-label">t('badge_full')</span>
</span>
```

- The label from `badge_full` ("AI use disclosed") as real text.
- The badge registers with the hover card (section 15), which shows the developer's notes on hover or focus. No `title`.

### Look

Built like the store's own tag chips, in our hue.

- `sar-badge-height` (19px) tall, `sar-badge-pad-inline` (7px), `sar-radius-sm`, no border.
- Label in `sar-text-badge` (11/19 regular, the page font rather than the chips' Arial, because the badge sits in Motiva rows), `sar-badge-fg` on `sar-badge-bg`.
- No icon. The violet hue and the wording set it apart from the store's blue tags, and it never sits in the store's tag row.
- Capped at `sar-badge-max-inline-size` (180px). English needs about 96px in the store font (82px of text plus padding), so a translation can run about 85% longer before it truncates; longer labels end in an ellipsis and keep the full text in the DOM.

### Placement

- Search rows: directly after the title, with `margin-inline-start: sar-space-2`, keeping its full size. The title line clips with `overflow: hidden`, so on a disclosed row it becomes a flex row: the title shrinks and ends in an ellipsis, and the badge stays visible. The platform icons below keep their place.
- Wishlist rows: directly after the title element, `margin-inline-start: sar-space-2`. The exact anchor is unverified until the surface spike.
- Any other row: after the title, never before it, so titles stay aligned.

### States

- Default only; its look does not change on hover. The row's own hover shows through the tint and the label still clears 5.21:1. Resting on it opens the hover card.
- `[data-sar-badges="off"]` on the root hides every badge in one style pass.
- Inside a dimmed host it dims with the host.

### Accessibility

- The label is real text, read as part of the row link even when truncated.
- No `role`, no `tabindex`: the badge rides inside the store's own link.

### Don't

- Don't say a game "uses AI", is "AI-free" or has "no AI", in any locale.
- Don't color it red, amber or green, and don't add a warning icon.

## 2. Compact badge

The overlay badge for capsule images, from 180px-wide capsules up to large header capsules, on an opaque surface so it reads over any art.

Preview: `previews/CompactBadge.html`.

### When to use

- A disclosed host whose capsule image is at least 180px wide and 32px tall. Smaller images get nothing on the image; a row-shaped host still gets the full badge.
- One compact badge per host, never alongside a full badge.

### What the consumer provides

```html
<span class="sar-compact" data-sar-size="text" data-sar-in-link="true">
  <span class="sar-compact-label">t('badge_compact')</span>
</span>
```

- `data-sar-size`, set once at placement from the measured image box (batched read, `getBoundingClientRect`). No container queries: they would change how store containers size themselves.
- `data-sar-in-link="true"` only when `badge.closest("a")` is the capsule link. The badge registers with the hover card either way; outside a link no pointer reaches it.
- `data-sar-anchor` on the image container if it computes to `position: static`; the stylesheet turns it into `position: relative`. Never an inline style.

### Size tiers

| Tier | Image box | Renders | Height | Type | Inset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| none | under 180px wide or 32px tall | nothing | | | |
| `text` | 180 to 399px wide | label | `sar-compact-height` 19px | `sar-text-compact` 11/17 medium | `sar-compact-inset-block` 5px, `sar-compact-inset-inline` 0 |
| `large` | 400px wide and 150px tall | label | `sar-compact-height-lg` 22px | `sar-text-compact-lg` 12/20 medium | same |

Tiers depend on image size only. A longer translation truncates inside its tier and never moves the badge to another.

### Look and placement

Built as a mirror of the store's own corner flags (in library, on wishlist): solid, square, 19px tall, 5px down from the top, flush to the edge.

- `sar-compact-fg` on `sar-compact-bg` (12.65:1 whatever the art), square corners, a 1px `sar-compact-border` and `sar-compact-shadow`.
- Unlike the store's mid-tone flags, the surface is dark (accent 900) and keeps an edge. Together they give every kind of art a boundary at 3:1 or more: the edge against black (5.91:1), the surface against white (16.63:1), mid grey (4.21:1) and violet (3.34:1).
- Block-start, inline-end corner (top right in English), mirroring the store's flags, which sit top-left. The store also owns the bottom edge (prices).
- `z-index: sar-z-badge`: over the art, the corner flags and the microtrailer video that home capsules play on hover, under the store's hover popups.
- Maximum inline size is the image width; past it the label ends in an ellipsis. Because the badge now shares the flags' row, a long translation on a small capsule can reach a store flag; see the open questions.

### Pointer and accessibility

- Inside the capsule link: `pointer-events: auto`, so hovering or clicking the badge is hovering or clicking the link, and the hover card opens. Focus on the link opens it too.
- Outside a link: `pointer-events: none`, so it never swallows a click on an overlaid link, and no hover card opens. The label stays in the DOM.

### Don't

- No translucent or blurred surface: contrast would depend on the art, and blur costs paint time on carousel pages.
- No rounded corners: the store's flags are square.
- Not bottom-left: cover art usually puts the game's logo there.

## 3. Summary row

One row in a game's summary box, directly under Publisher, that says the developer disclosed AI use with a violet tag-styled chip and jumps to the banner.

Preview: `previews/SummaryRow.html`.

### When to use

- On the app page of a disclosed game while the `badges` setting is on. Allow-listed, not-disclosed and unknown games get nothing.
- Once per page, in the summary box at the top right, so the disclosure is visible above the fold. The banner, lower down by the purchase box, still carries the developer's words.

### What the consumer provides

```html
<a class="sar-summary-row" href="#sar-banner">
  <span class="sar-summary-label">t('summary_label')</span>
  <span class="sar-badge">
    <span class="sar-badge-label">t('summary_value')</span>
  </span>
</a>
```

- Insert it right after the last `.dev_row` in the summary box (`.glance_ctn_responsive_left`). Steam's own rows there are label and value pairs; this row follows the same shape with its own classes, and never borrows or restyles a store node.
- With the banner on, the row is a progressive link: `href="#sar-banner"` (the banner host's id), handled by one delegated click listener that calls `preventDefault`, scrolls the host into view with `block: "start"`, and focuses the banner's `aside` (`tabindex="-1"`) with `focus({ preventScroll: true })`. The URL never changes, so there is no history entry and no `#sar-banner` in links people copy. Without the script, the plain anchor still lands on the banner. Steam's own reviews row jumps to the reviews section the same way.
- With the banner off, render the row as a `div` with no `href` and `tabindex="0"`: the chip still says what it says, and the hover card (section 15) quotes the notes. With the banner on, the row opens no hover card: the jump lands on the full notes.

### Look

Steam's summary box is set in Arial (13/18 values, 11/18 uppercase labels), and its tag chips are 11/19 Arial. This row matches it.

- Label column `sar-summary-label-inline-size` (35% of the row, like the store's labels), `sar-summary-label-pad-inline-end` (10px), `sar-text-summary-label`, uppercase, in `sar-color-text-subtle`. The store's own label color, `#798a95`, is 4.18:1 on the page, so ours is a step lighter to pass AA.
- Value: the full badge (`sar-badge-fg` on `sar-badge-bg`, 19px, `sar-radius-sm`), set in `sar-text-summary-tag` like the store's tag chips. A violet chip in a box of blue links is what draws the eye.
- The chip may use the whole value column; past it the label ends in an ellipsis, and the full text stays in the DOM.
- Interaction: a pointer cursor and no underline, like the store's own review rows, plus the shared `sar-focus-color` ring on `:focus-visible`, which the store's rows lack.

### Fit

Measured on a 1280 by 900 window (app 1808500): the row is 18px tall, the summary box has slack, so the tag row below moves down 5px and no store tag is hidden. The worst case is a game with two review rows and a publisher name that wraps, about 36px more; the tag row would then hang past the media's bottom edge (misaligned, not clipped). If that shows up, drop the row on that page; the banner still carries the disclosure.

### Accessibility

- The link's name is the label plus the chip text, for example "AI use: Disclosed by the developer".
- The catalog strings stay in sentence case. The uppercase is `text-transform`, which follows the page's `lang`, so locales such as Turkish case correctly.
- The jump lands on the banner, and focus follows, so a keyboard or screen reader user arrives at the developer's words.
- With the banner off, focus on the row opens the hover card, which describes the row while it is open.

### Don't

- Don't add it to Steam's tag row: that row never wraps, Steam's own script hides tags that don't fit, and a chip there would read as a player-defined tag.
- Don't float it over the page.
- Don't color the label or add a warning icon; the chip is the only violet.

## 4. App page banner

A non-blocking notice on a game's own store page that quotes the developer's AI disclosure verbatim, placed above the purchase box and built like the store's own notice bubbles.

Preview: `previews/Banner.html`.

### When to use

- On the app page of a disclosed game, once per page view, while the `banner` setting is on. An allow-listed game gets the one-line note instead.
- Never as a modal or overlay, never over content, never stealing focus.

### What the consumer provides

```html
<div class="sar-banner-host">
  #shadow-root
  <aside class="sar-banner" aria-labelledby="sar-banner-title">
    <div class="sar-banner-head">
      <h2 class="sar-banner-title" id="sar-banner-title">t('banner_title')</h2>
      <button class="sar-banner-action" type="button">t('banner_ignore')</button>
    </div>
    <p class="sar-banner-source">t('banner_source')</p>
    <blockquote class="sar-banner-quote" id="sar-banner-notes" data-sar-collapsed="true">notes</blockquote>
    <button class="sar-banner-link" type="button" aria-expanded="false"
            aria-controls="sar-banner-notes">t('banner_expand')</button>
    <p class="sar-banner-service">t('banner_service_label') <span class="sar-banner-service-name">name</span></p>
    <p class="sar-banner-error" role="alert" hidden>t('error_allow_list_save')</p>
    <div class="sar-vh" aria-live="polite"></div>
  </aside>
</div>
```

- The notes as data: `textContent`, `white-space: pre-line`, never translated, summarized or highlighted.
- The named AI service as plain text, never a link. No service: omit the line.
- The host carries `id="sar-banner"`, the target of the summary row's link, and the `aside` carries `tabindex="-1"` so focus can follow that jump.
- A shadow root on `div.sar-banner-host`, styles attached with `adoptedStyleSheets`. Tokens inherit from `:root`, and the page font works inside because `@font-face` is document-wide.

### Placement

First child of the left description column, directly above the purchase box: where a buyer looks before buying. Full column width, about 616 to 643px on desktop and down to about 360px in the store's narrow layout. Re-inserted only if a store re-render removes it.

### Look

Modeled on the store's per-app notice bubbles (the DLC notice sits in the same slot): a hue-coded wash and nothing else. Every per-app notice on the store is a gradient in its own hue, so a colored bubble is the native idiom; our hue keeps it from passing as the store's own content.

- Background `sar-banner-bg`: a 135 degree wash from `sar-banner-wash-start` at 10% to `sar-banner-wash-end` at 100% (accent 800 to accent 600, both at 80%), the DLC bubble's recipe in our hue.
- No border, no radius, no shadow.
- Padding `sar-space-2` at block-start, `sar-space-3` at block-end, `sar-space-4` inline. `sar-space-4` below: the store's own 2px would glue the banner to the purchase box and read as part of the offer.
- All text in `sar-banner-fg`. Title `sar-text-banner-title` (24/30 light). Source and service lines `sar-text-banner-meta` (13/19 light). Notes `sar-text-banner-quote` (14/21, italic like the notes in the store's own disclosure section).
- "Stop marking this game" is the store's white-transparent button: `sar-text-banner-button` (15/30 light) in `sar-banner-button-fg`, `sar-banner-button-pad-inline`, a 1px `sar-banner-button-border`, `sar-radius-sm`, a `sar-banner-button-bg-hover` wash on hover.
- "Show full disclosure" and "Undo" are the store's underlined in-bubble links, in `sar-banner-link-color`, as `button` elements with a 24px minimum target.
- Errors in `sar-banner-error`, never `sar-color-error` (4.21:1 on the bright end).
- Never `sar-color-text-muted` or `sar-color-text-subtle` on the wash.

### States

- **Collapsed** (default when long): the quote clamps to `sar-banner-quote-lines` (3). Measure in the next frame; if it does not overflow, drop `data-sar-collapsed` and omit the link.
- **Expanded:** the link reads `banner_collapse` with `aria-expanded="true"`. No animation; not remembered across pages.
- **Allow-listed** (`data-sar-variant="ignored"`): the store's DRM-notice geometry in neutral, no violet. One line: `banner_ignored` in `sar-text-banner-note` and `sar-color-text-muted`, and an Undo link, on `sar-banner-note-bg` with `sar-radius-xs`, `sar-space-1` by `sar-space-2` padding, labelled by `banner_region_label`.
- **Storage error:** the banner stays as it was and shows `error_allow_list_save` until the next successful action.
- **Setting turned off:** remove the host. Turned on: re-render from cache, no new request.

### Behavior

- "Stop marking this game" adds the app ID (with the name and today's date) to the allow-list and swaps in the one-line note; focus moves to Undo. It never calls the store's own ignore feature.
- "Undo" restores the full banner, collapsed; focus returns to "Stop marking this game".
- The polite live region announces `notice_marking_stopped` or `notice_marking_restored`.

### Longer translations

## 5. Dim and hide (list filter)

The opt-in dim or hide mode for lists, written as pure CSS on two attributes so a setting change is one write, however many games a page holds.

Preview: `previews/ListFilter.html`.

### What the consumer provides

- On `document.documentElement`: `data-sar-filter="off" | "dim" | "hide"` (default `off`).
- On each host, written once by the scanner: `data-sar-state` (`disclosed`, `not-disclosed`, `unknown`, `allowed`) and, where hiding would break a virtualized or carousel list, `data-sar-hideable="false"`.

No classes, no inline styles, no per-node writes when the mode changes.

### Behavior

- `off`: lists unchanged; badges only.
- `dim`: disclosed hosts get `filter: opacity(sar-dim-opacity)` (0.4). `:hover` and `:focus-within` restore full opacity on that one host with a `sar-duration-fast` fade in and a snap back.
- `hide`: disclosed hosts get `display: none`. Hosts marked `data-sar-hideable="false"` dim instead.
- Allowed, not-disclosed and unknown hosts never match.

```css
[data-sar-filter="dim"] [data-sar-state="disclosed"],
[data-sar-filter="hide"] [data-sar-state="disclosed"][data-sar-hideable="false"] {
  filter: opacity(var(--sar-dim-opacity)) !important;
}
[data-sar-filter="hide"] [data-sar-state="disclosed"]:not([data-sar-hideable="false"]) {
  display: none !important;
}
```

### Rules

- A filter opacity only. It multiplies with the store's own `opacity`, while the `opacity` property would replace it: the home page's main carousel hides its other slides with `opacity: 0`, and an `opacity` dim made them show through. It costs one effect per dimmed host, as `opacity` does. Other filters such as grayscale cost more; a sale page holds hundreds of capsules, so profile before adding one.
- No transition on a mode change, so every host flips in one style pass.
- `!important` is required: store rows set `display` inline or with higher specificity.
- The store's own ignored state is 0.3. Ours is 0.4, and the badge on the host names the reason.
- Dimmed content is inactive UI: exempt from contrast minimums, never the only route to information.
- Known gap: the filter can apply after first paint, because the content script runs at `document_idle`.

## 12. Backup

Saves everything the extension keeps to a file the person holds, and restores it after a reinstall or in another browser: there are no accounts and no server copy, so the file is the backup.

Preview: `previews/Backup.html`.

### What a backup holds

- Settings: badges, banner, list filter, language.
- Games you stopped marking, with their names and dates.
- Lookup history: every game already checked, marked or not, with when it was checked, so a restored history still expires on its usual schedule.

One JSON file named `ai-reporter-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json`, with a format version. Its schema lives with the storage code. Nothing else goes in: no cookies, no account data, nothing from the store.

### Where it appears

- The full options page: its own card, after "Games you stopped marking" and before "Privacy".
- The userscript settings dialog: the same card.
- Not the toolbar popup: opening a file picker can close an extension popup before the file arrives. The popup's "All settings" link leads to the full page.

### What the consumer provides

```html
<section class="sar-card" aria-labelledby="backup-title">
  <h2 class="sar-card-title" id="backup-title">t('options_section_backup')</h2>
  <p class="sar-backup-help">t('options_backup_help')</p>
  <div class="sar-backup-actions">
    <button class="sar-button" type="button">t('options_backup_download')</button>
    <button class="sar-button" type="button">t('options_backup_restore')</button>
    <input type="file" accept=".json,application/json" hidden>
  </div>

  <!-- Only after a valid file is chosen -->
  <div class="sar-backup-summary" role="group" aria-labelledby="backup-summary-title">
    <p class="sar-backup-summary-title" id="backup-summary-title" tabindex="-1">
      t('options_backup_summary_title', { date })
    </p>
    <ul class="sar-backup-contents">
      <li>t('options_backup_summary_settings')</li>
      <li>t('options_backup_summary_allow', { count })</li>
      <li>t('options_backup_summary_history', { count })</li>
    </ul>
    <p class="sar-backup-note">t('options_backup_restore_note')</p>
    <div class="sar-backup-actions">
      <button class="sar-button" data-sar-variant="primary" type="button">t('options_backup_confirm')</button>
      <button class="sar-button" type="button">t('options_backup_cancel')</button>
    </div>
  </div>

  <p class="sar-backup-status" hidden>t('notice_backup_restored')</p>
  <p class="sar-field-error" role="alert" hidden>t('error_backup_invalid', { name })</p>
  <div class="sar-vh" aria-live="polite"></div>
</section>
```

- "Restore from a backup" is a real `button` that calls `input.click()` on a hidden file input. A styled `label` cannot take keyboard focus.
- Counts and the date come from the file after it is validated; the date is formatted with `Intl.DateTimeFormat(locale, { dateStyle: "medium" })`. Counts use plural keys (`_one`, `_other`).

### Flow

**Download**

1. "Download backup" builds the file from storage and starts the download (an `a[download]` on a Blob URL, revoked afterwards). The browser's own download UI confirms the file; the live region announces `notice_backup_downloaded`.
2. If the file cannot be built: `error_backup_create`. Nothing else changes.

**Restore**

1. "Restore from a backup" opens the file picker.
2. The file is untrusted data: cap its size, parse it as JSON, validate it against the schema, and never render any of its text as HTML. Game names go in with `textContent`.
3. Not a backup: `error_backup_invalid`. From a newer format: `error_backup_newer`. Unreadable: `error_backup_read`. Nothing changes in any of these.
4. Valid: the summary opens in place, inside the card. No modal. Focus moves to its title, so a screen reader hears the date and contents.
5. "Restore" applies everything in one write: settings are replaced; games you stopped marking are merged (the earlier date wins); history is merged (the newer check wins). Open store tabs update through the settings subscription, as for any settings change.
6. Afterwards the summary closes, the status line reads `notice_backup_restored`, the live region announces it, and focus returns to "Restore from a backup".
7. If the write fails: `error_backup_restore`. Nothing is half-applied.
8. "Cancel" closes the summary and returns focus to "Restore from a backup".

### Look

- Help text in `sar-text-help` and `sar-color-text-subtle`.
- Actions: a wrapping row of buttons, `sar-space-2` apart. Long labels wrap inside their buttons, and buttons drop to the next line when the row is too narrow.
- Summary: `sar-color-bg-sunken`, 1px `sar-color-border`, `sar-radius-md`, `sar-space-3` padding. Title in `sar-color-text-strong`, medium. Contents in `sar-color-text`. The note in `sar-text-help` and `sar-color-text-subtle`.
- "Restore" is the one primary button in the product (`data-sar-variant="primary"`): it confirms the only action that overwrites settings.
- The status line uses `sar-text-help` in `sar-color-text`. Errors use `.sar-field-error`.
- No green for success and no red fill for failure: words carry the state.

### Don't

- Don't restore the moment a file is picked. Always show the summary first.
- Don't send the file anywhere or read it on a server.
- Don't put a backup control on store pages or in the popup.

## 15. Hover card

The developer's disclosure in a small dark panel that opens when a pointer rests on a badge, or when keyboard focus reaches one. Badges carry no `title`; the card is their only tooltip.

Preview: `previews/HoverCard.html`.

### When to use

- On the full badge, on a compact badge inside the capsule link, and on the summary row while the banner is off. These are its triggers.
- Not on the summary row while the banner is on: its click jumps to the full notes, and a card there would cover the tag row on the way to that click.
- Not on a compact badge outside a link: it keeps `pointer-events: none` (section 2) and nothing opens.
- Not on the banner, which quotes the notes in full.

### What the consumer provides

```html
<div class="sar-hovercard" id="sar-hovercard" role="tooltip" data-sar-side="below" hidden>
  <p class="sar-hovercard-title">t('badge_card_title')</p>
  <p class="sar-hovercard-source">t('badge_card_source')</p>
  <p class="sar-hovercard-notes">notes, verbatim</p>
  <p class="sar-hovercard-service">t('badge_card_service_label') <span class="sar-hovercard-service-name">service</span></p>
  <p class="sar-hovercard-more" hidden>t('badge_card_more')</p>
</div>
```

- One card per page, created on first use and appended to `body`. Every trigger reuses it; opening for another game refills it.
- Triggers register with the card when they render: a `WeakMap` from trigger element to verdict, plus whether the trigger sits on the game's own page (the summary row). Nothing is set on the trigger, and no listener is added to it.
- Opening and closing run on delegated listeners on the document: `pointerover`, `pointerout`, `focusin`, `focusout`, `keydown` and `scroll`. Their number does not depend on how many badges the page holds.
- The notes go in through `textContent` with `\r\n` turned into `\n`, never translated or cut in the DOM. The service line appears only when GetItems named one (`ai_generation_service`), the name inserted as data.
- No request: the card shows the verdict the badge was drawn from.

### Look

Built like the store's game hover popup, sampled on the home page on 2026-10-21: a `#344352` panel, no radius, a 20px black shadow at 50%, 16px of padding, 306px wide, set in the page font.

- Surface `sar-hovercard-bg` with `sar-hovercard-shadow`, padding `sar-hovercard-pad`, inline size `sar-hovercard-inline-size`, capped at the viewport width less two offsets.
- Title in `sar-hovercard-title-font` (15/17, like the store's game name there), `sar-badge-fg`. The violet title is what marks the card as ours.
- Source line in `sar-hovercard-meta-font` (11/16), `sar-color-text-muted`, like the store's release line.
- Notes in `sar-hovercard-notes-font` (12/18 italic, like the store's own disclosure section), `sar-color-text`, `white-space: pre-line`, clamped to `sar-hovercard-notes-lines` (6) lines.
- Service line in the meta font: label `sar-color-text-muted`, name `sar-color-text`.
- The "more" line, `badge_card_more` in the meta font and `sar-color-text-muted`, shows only when the clamp hides text and the trigger is not on the game's own page. The app page carries Steam's own disclosure section and, when on, the banner; the summary row's card never shows the line. The full notes stay in the DOM for screen readers, and the game's store page carries them in Steam's own disclosure section.
- No arrow. The store's popup has a bitmap arrow toward the capsule; ours sits right under the badge, and the short gap ties it to its trigger.

### Placement

- Fixed to the viewport, `sar-hovercard-offset` (8px) below the trigger. Where it would cross the bottom edge and fits above, it flips above (`data-sar-side="above"`).
- Horizontally, the full badge and the summary row (banner off) align the card's inline-start edge with their own. A compact badge aligns the inline-end edges: the card hangs back over the capsule art, away from the side where the store's popup opens. Either way the card stays one offset inside the viewport edges.
- `z-index: sar-z-hovercard` (2100), above the store's game hover popup (1526) and the full-viewport layer that React store pages (tag hubs, categories) mount for their capsule hover (2000). Over a capsule both open: the store's popup beside the capsule, ours under the badge. Where the store's popup flips to the same side, ours is on top, because the pointer is on our badge.
- The card covers store content only while it is open, and only in answer to a pointer or focus on our own element. It never opens by itself.

### Behavior

- Pointer: opens after 150ms at rest on a trigger. A pointer crossing a grid of capsules opens nothing. Moving to another trigger while it is open switches at once.
- The card is hoverable: leaving the trigger starts a 100ms grace, long enough for the pointer to cross onto the card, which then stays open. Leaving both, or the window, closes it. Both delays are timers in `hover-card.ts`, not tokens.
- Keyboard: opens at once when focus lands on the summary row (banner off) or on a store link that holds a badge (a search row or a capsule link). Moving focus elsewhere closes it.
- `Escape` closes it, and it stays closed until the pointer leaves the trigger or focus moves.
- Any scroll outside the card closes a card the pointer opened; a fixed card would drift away from its trigger. A card focus opened follows its trigger instead, because focus scrolls a link into view: the one delegated scroll listener queues at most one move per frame, which reads only the trigger's box, and the card closes once the trigger leaves the viewport.
- `[data-sar-badges="off"]` hides it, like every badge.

### Accessibility

- `role="tooltip"` on the card. While focus opened it, the focused element gets `aria-describedby="sar-hovercard"`, added to any ids already there and restored on close. On a store link this is the one store-node attribute outside `data-sar-*`, and it lasts only while the card is open.
- The summary row is always focusable: a link with the banner on, `tabindex="0"` on the `div` without it. Keyboard users reach the notes either way.
- Badges stay unfocusable; focus rides on the store's link around them.
- WCAG 2.2 content on hover or focus (1.4.13): dismissible with `Escape`, hoverable, and open until the pointer or focus leaves.

### Motion

- Fades in over `sar-duration-fast` with `sar-ease` through `@starting-style`; closing is instant. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` sets the duration to 0ms and the card appears without a fade.
- No movement: position and size never animate.

### Don't

- Don't add a link or button inside the card. Keyboard users never reach it, because focus stays on the trigger.
- Don't fetch anything to fill it.
- Don't add per-badge listeners or a card per badge.

### Limits that keep these true

- On the banner wash, text is `sar-banner-fg` only. `sar-color-text-muted` is 3.77:1 at the bright end, `sar-color-error` 4.21:1, and the store's own in-bubble link color `#c3c1c0` 4.49:1.
- The banner button's edge is white at 50%. The store's own 40% would be 2.63:1 on our bright end.
- The store's own summary label color, `#798a95`, is 4.18:1 on the page, so the summary row label uses `sar-color-text-subtle` instead.
- `sar-color-text-subtle` only on page, row, panel, raised and sunken. On the store's row hover it is 3.32:1.
- `sar-accent-300` as link text only on solid grounds. On the store's row hover it is 4.45:1, so links there use `sar-accent-200`.
- The compact badge's surface stays at accent 900. A mid-tone flag color such as accent 700 would leave mid grey art with no boundary at 3:1 (surface 2.54, edge 1.11) and violet art at 2.01.
- The switch's off track is 1.55:1 against raised; its `sar-toggle-track-off-border` edge carries the 3:1.
