## 2026.11.10 - 2026-11-10
### Added
- The All Settings page has a Go back to Steam button that returns to the store tab you opened it from, or opens the Steam store when that tab is gone.
- Tags now show in the discovery queue player on the store home page, under each game's name and price, and they change with the game as you move through the queue.
- The userscript now links to the project's home page and issue tracker from your userscript manager, and shows the Tag Kit icon there.
### Changed
- The Steam Deck and Steam Machine settings now take one rating to tag (Verified, Playable or Unsupported), since a game has one rating per device. Settings and backups from earlier versions keep the first rating they ticked, in that order, or Unsupported when none was ticked.
- The settings tabs, the popup and the Tag order card name each tag by a short name, with a line that describes it, and the tags' tabs sit under a Tags caption after General. The Tag order card now marks where game images, search suggestions and search, wishlist and DLC rows run out of room.
- The project moved to github.com/jrv97/tag-kit-for-steam. The bug report and feedback links point there, the store zips and the userscript files are named tag-kit-for-steam, and the userscript is named Tag Kit for Steam. Backups are saved as tag-kit-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json, and backups saved by earlier versions no longer restore.
- The extension icon draws its tags with a thinner line and hangs them lower on the Steam badge.
- The feedback links at the bottom of the settings start with violet line icons in place of emoji.
- On a game page, "Ignore tags for this game" ignores every tag on the game and its DLC, and hovering or clicking its arrow opens one menu with every other ignore (the game, each tag, its developers and its publishers) as checkboxes that undo when unchecked.
### Fixed
- A click on the toolbar popup's gear or on a tag's name right after the popup opens now opens the settings and closes the popup, the same as a later click.
- Hovering a tag on the shown capsule of a home page carousel opens that game's card again. A hidden slide stacked under it caught the pointer, so the card showed another game's tags and a click opened that hidden game.
- The settings page no longer shows a scrollbar when the open tab fits in the window. A long tab scrolls inside its panel while the header, the tab list and the footer stay in place.
- Failures that used to hide behind a default now reach the browser console: a detection source that breaks, browser storage that cannot read or save the pause after Steam refuses a request (logged once until storage works again), and settings that cannot be read when the language is picked.
