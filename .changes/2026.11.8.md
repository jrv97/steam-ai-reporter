## 2026.11.8 - 2026-11-08
### Changed
- The settings page labels the Family Sharing choices "Available" and "Not Available", and the Spanish Tag order help says "arrastra y suelta".
- The settings tabs and the popup rows follow the tag order, and the tabs read Steam's headings. With no saved order the tags now sort alphabetically by Steam's heading, so the first two chips on small images change.
- The Tag order card marks where small images stop showing tags with a divider and one line of text, replacing the "small image" label on each row.
- The settings page and the userscript dialog keep the header, the tab rail and the feedback bar in place and scroll only the open panel.
### Fixed
- The banner button reads "Ignore tag for this game", and the Spanish chip accessible name uses a comma after the chip text.
- On the settings tabs, the arrow keys now move focus between tabs and Enter or Space selects the focused tab. Before, Enter did nothing, Space toggled something else, and the arrow keys stopped working after a click on blank space in the panel.
- In the toolbar popup, clicking the gear now closes the popup and opens the settings page, the same as clicking a tag's name.
- Turning a tag off on its settings tab no longer hides its other controls. The values, badge and banner switches, chip text, color and list filter stay in place, dimmed and disabled, so the page does not shift.
